## 6. Exactly what the new source APIs do — and DO NOT do

The optional staged `CompileNPCs(stage)`, `CompileObjects(stage)`, `CompileQuests(stage)`, `CompileItems(stage)` and `CompileTableCoroutine(...,stage)` produce caller-owned scratch `Bin/Ptrs` pairs. Optional writer `stage._nqWriterLeaseOwner` registers/finalises streams; optional sixth `GetDBHandle(...,readerLeaseOwner)` registers reader stream **before** yield; an external coroutine driver owns DEAD/suspended state.

Real `Questie.lua:115–117` defines `Questie:Error` to **print, not throw**. #184 fixes the staged invalid-datatype early-return failure to prevent a false apparent SUCCESS. #185 adds a *current-attempt scratch receipt* for each compiled pair; verifies all four pairs, refuses prior raw bytes without current receipts, and leaves SavedVariables unchanged. #186 captures a token **and serial** at writer entry; after a coroutine yield, a previously suspended writer cannot stamp a newer scratch attempt, including visible token A→B→A.

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION:** #186's serial is a **scratch-generation** fence, **not a source-writer epoch**. It does not protect arbitrary writes to `QuestieDB.questData`, `npcData`, `objectData`, `itemData`, nested arrays or faction overrides across yields. It cannot detect source A→B→A unless every relevant writer is instrumented or input is proven immutable.
